Chrome does not let extensions change the default search engine on Linux or ChromeOS. You can add it once yourself:

  1. Open the address bar and go to chrome://settings/searchEngines.
  2. Under Site search, choose Add.
  3. Fill in the fields below, save, then pick Make default.
